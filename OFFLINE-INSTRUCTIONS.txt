OFFLINE RAFFLE INSTRUCTIONS

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP file and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser without internet access.
4. Select Upload Excel or CSV.
5. Choose a locally saved participant file that is closed in Excel.

Accepted formats: .xlsx, .xls, .xlsm, .xlsb, and .csv
Recommended columns: Badge No, Name, Title

Keep the vendor folder beside index.html. The offline page needs that folder to read spreadsheets.
